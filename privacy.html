Privacy Policy

Bookshelf Club · Effective date: September 22, 2026
Developer and data controller: Mert Aydin · smertaydin@me.com

1. Information we process

We process account identifiers, your email address (including an Apple relay address if you choose it), and the display name you provide. When you use the app, we store your library, reading status, page progress, reading dates and activity, ratings, notes, saved quotes and their book/page/style metadata. Social features store friend relationships, sharing preferences, club membership and roles, suggestions, votes, reviews, meeting details and attendance responses.

Our authentication and hosting providers may process technical connection data such as IP addresses, request timestamps and security logs to operate and protect the service. If you contact us, we receive your message and contact details. TestFlight feedback and diagnostics may be made available to us by Apple according to your settings and Apple's terms.

2. Why we use this information

We use these data to authenticate users, synchronize reading records, calculate reading activity, provide the social features you use, respond to support requests and protect the service. Necessary account and feature processing supports providing the service you request. Optional camera and notification features depend on your choices and device permissions. Security and support processing supports our legitimate interest in operating a reliable service, subject to applicable law.

3. Photos, quotes and device permissions

Camera access is used for scanning book barcodes and capturing passages. You can select an individual photo through the system photo picker. Quote text recognition runs on your device using Apple's frameworks. We do not upload these source photos to our backend or send them to a third-party AI service. When you save a quote, its text, private note and associated metadata are stored in your account.

Exported quote images are generated locally and sent to the destination you choose using the system share sheet; private quote notes are excluded from the exported design. Temporary export files may remain on your device until app or system cleanup. Notifications are optional local reminders. You may change camera and notification permissions in iOS Settings.

4. Sharing and service providers

Friends can see the books and reading information made available through your sharing settings. Club members can see information you contribute to their club, including reading progress, votes where exposed by the club interface, reviews and attendance. Reviews linked to a club reading may synchronize with the corresponding personal-library review. Saved quotes are private within the app unless you export and share them yourself. Recipients may retain copies you share outside the app.

We use Supabase for authentication and database hosting. Apple provides Sign in with Apple and TestFlight. Book searches and cover requests use Google Books and Open Library; these providers receive the search terms or book identifiers and normal network request information. We do not send your private notes or quotes to book catalogue providers.

Service providers process data under their applicable terms and privacy protections. Data may be processed outside your country where those providers operate, with applicable safeguards. See Supabase privacy, Apple privacy, Google privacy and Internet Archive / Open Library terms and privacy.

We do not sell your personal data, use it for targeted advertising, or include advertising tracking SDKs in the current app. We may disclose information when required by law or as necessary to protect users and the service.

5. Retention, deletion and your choices

Account and reading data are retained while your account is active and needed to provide the service. You can edit or remove content where the app offers those controls. Removing a book from your library does not automatically remove its saved quotes; delete those separately if you want them removed.

To request access, correction, an export, or deletion of your account and associated personal data, email smertaydin@me.com. We may verify account ownership before acting. The current beta does not include an in-app account deletion button. After a verified deletion request, we remove associated personal data from active systems, subject to legal obligations and security needs. Residual copies may remain in provider backups or security logs until their normal retention cycles expire; they are not used to restore your active account. Content already exported or copied by others is outside our control.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent for optional processing, and contact your local data protection authority. Withdrawing an optional permission does not undo earlier lawful processing.

6. Security

We use encrypted network connections, account authentication and database access controls. Authentication sessions are stored in the iOS Keychain. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

8. This website and policy updates

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. GitHub may process visitor connection data for hosting and security under its privacy statement. We will publish changes to this policy here and update the effective date; material changes will be communicated as appropriate.
